Why we built it

A lead site makes money when you chase work. We only make money when you win it.

That one difference decides every rule on this page. It’s worth seeing where it leads, because the incentive is doing the work — not the promise.

Paid per lead — what makes them money

  • →Selling one job to as many trades as possible. Every extra tradie chasing it is another fee.
  • →A job that’s hard to win. You pay to quote either way, so losing costs them nothing.
  • →Urgency. The faster you feel you must buy the lead, the less you weigh up whether it’s worth it.
  • →Staying put. A long term means the money keeps coming whether the work does or not.

Paid a flat fee — what makes us money

  • ✓You winning enough to stay. It’s the only thing that pays us, so it’s what the rules are tuned for.
  • ✓Capping a job at {{ cfg.MAX_QUOTES }} quotes. Fewer people chasing it is worse for a lead site and better for you.
  • ✓Sending you jobs you can actually do — your trade, your area, in turn. A job you can’t win wastes the hour you spent on it.
  • ✓Letting you leave. Month to month, one click. If we stop earning you work we should stop being paid.

We’re not claiming to be nicer people. We’ve just set it up so the thing that’s good for you is the thing that pays us — and where that isn’t true, we’d rather say so than pretend. Posting a job is free for homeowners and always will be; that side of it doesn’t pay us anything, and it isn’t meant to.

How it works

If you need a job done

  1. Describe the job

    What needs doing, a rough budget, and photos if you have them. Free, and about five minutes.

  2. Up to {{ cfg.MAX_QUOTES }} quotes arrive

    Each quote has the price, what’s included and what isn’t, the warranty and when they can start.

  3. Compare, then share your number

    Trades see your suburb, not your address or phone. Share your details only with the trades you want to talk to.

  4. Hire and review

    Accept a quote and get the job done. Only customers who hired a trade can review them.

If you’re a trade

  1. Pick a plan by job size

    ${{ tiers.small.price }}, ${{ tiers.medium.price }} or ${{ tiers.large.price }} a month. Each plan includes every job below it. No tokens and no lead fees.

  2. Get offered jobs in turn

    Fair rotation shares out jobs in your trade and area. You’re one of {{ cfg.TRADES_PER_JOB }}, not one of 50.

  3. Quote within {{ cfg.OFFER_WINDOW_HOURS }} hours

    Send a structured quote from your phone. Not for you? Pass, and it goes to another trade straight away.

  4. Win work, or get the month back

    Quote on five jobs in a month and don’t win one, and we refund that month.

What you get, beyond the quotes

Finding the work is the start. Most of what goes wrong on a residential job happens after that, so the job carries on here — on the website and on the phone, for both of you.

Quoting

Break the price down

Optional line items — labour, materials, rubbish — that add up to the total. Customers ask where the money goes; this answers it before they have to.

Talking

Messages, photos and files

Send a photo of what you found under the boards, or a plan set. Only the two of you can open it — attachments never sit in a public folder.

Variations

Extra work, agreed in writing

Found three rotten joists? Send it across with a price. They press yes or no and the answer is kept. Verbal extras are the commonest thing people fall out over.

On site

Access notes and pre-starts

Where to park, which gate, is the dog in. Plus a pre-start check on the day, so the hazards get looked at rather than assumed.

While it runs

Progress updates

A photo and a line at the end of the day. The customer stops ringing to ask how it’s going, which is worth more than it sounds.

Questions

What does it cost to post a job?

Nothing. Posting, getting quotes and messaging trades is free.

Why only {{ cfg.MAX_QUOTES }} quotes?

{{ cfg.MAX_QUOTES }} is enough to compare prices and approaches without spending a week on the phone. It also means every trade who quotes has a real chance of winning, so they put more into their quotes — and you still hear back faster than ringing around yourself.

What if nobody quotes?

Unused slots go to new trades as each {{ cfg.OFFER_WINDOW_HOURS }}-window runs out. That carries on for {{ cfg.JOB_OPEN_DAYS }} days, or until you have {{ cfg.MAX_QUOTES }} quotes.

Do trades see my address and phone number?

No. Trades see the job details and your suburb. Your name, phone, email and street address are only shared with trades you choose, or the trade whose quote you accept.

How do you check trades?

Trades give us their licence or registration number, NZBN and insurance details, and we check them against the public registers and their certificates. A badge only appears once a check is done, and it tells you what we checked and when.

How are jobs shared out between trades?

By rotation. Among trades who do that work in that area, on a plan that covers the job’s size, the ones offered the fewest similar jobs recently go first. Reviews and ratings play no part.

What counts as winning a job, for the refund?

A customer accepting your quote on {{ cfg.BRAND }} during that billing month. Read the full guarantee terms.
