What this can and can’t do

  • It only ever creates jobs in your Docket. It can’t read your clients, your staff, your timesheets or anything else, and it can’t delete a thing.
  • It only sends jobs you’ve won. Nothing goes across about a job you merely quoted on.
  • Sending twice is safe. If we’re not sure a job arrived we send it again, and Docket updates the one that’s there rather than making a second.
  • If Docket is down, nothing here breaks. We keep trying in the background, and you can send a job by hand from the job itself.
