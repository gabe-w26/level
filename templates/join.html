Why there’s a waiting list and not a sign-up button

Nobody’s proud of a job they had to come back and redo. We’re building this the way you’d build anything you put your name on — right the first time, rather than fast now and patched later.

Opening an area with three tradies in it would technically work. You’d post a job, and it would sit there with no quotes. That’s the one impression we don’t get a second go at, so we’d rather tell you we’re not ready than waste your week proving it.

  • An area opens when there’s enough cover to do the work — not when the marketing is ready.
  • Nothing ships half-built. No beta badges, no buttons that say “coming soon”, no features that only work if you don’t look too hard.
  • Where something isn’t right, the page says so. Our own price comparison admits the plan where we’re not the cheapest, because a table that only ever wins isn’t worth reading.

It means we’re slower to open than we’d like. It also means that when your area does open, it works.
