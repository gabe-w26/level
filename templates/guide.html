What changes the price

  • How big the job is, and how much of it has to be pulled out first
  • Access — stairs, tight spaces, height and parking all add time
  • Materials you choose, and whether they’re in stock
  • Surprises once work starts, like rot or old wiring — ask how they’re priced
  • How soon you need it done

Before you say yes

  • Get the price, what’s included and what isn’t, in writing
  • Ask about the warranty on their workmanship
  • Agree when payments are due — pay as work is done, not a large deposit up front
  • For residential work of {{ cfg.CONTRACT_THRESHOLD|money }} or more, you’re owed a written contract, a disclosure statement and MBIE’s checklist
