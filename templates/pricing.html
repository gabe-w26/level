The no-job guarantee

If you don’t win a job in a billing month, we refund that month.

It’s there so trying {{ cfg.BRAND }} isn’t a gamble. Here’s exactly how it works.

  1. Send at least {{ cfg.GUARANTEE_MIN_QUOTES }} quotes in the month. If we offered you fewer than {{ cfg.GUARANTEE_MIN_QUOTES }} jobs, quote on every job we offered. If we offered you none, you qualify automatically.
  2. Winning a job means a customer accepted your quote on {{ cfg.BRAND }} during that billing month.
  3. If you pause new jobs at any point in the month, you need the full {{ cfg.GUARANTEE_MIN_QUOTES }} quotes, however many jobs you were offered.
  4. Quotes must be genuine. A quote a customer reports as not genuine, and that we agree isn’t, doesn’t count.
  5. We check every month when it ends and refund the full month to the card you paid with. There’s no claim form and nothing to ask for — if you qualify, it’s refunded. You’ll see the result on your Plan page.

Plan questions

Is there a contract?

No. Plans are month to month. Cancel online in one click and you keep getting jobs until the end of the month you’ve paid for.

Can I change plans?

Any time, from your Plan page. The change applies straight away.

How many jobs will I get?

That depends on how many jobs are posted in your trade and area. Your dashboard shows how many you were offered next to the average for trades on your plan in your trade and area, so you can see the rotation is fair.

What if I’m flat out?

Pause new jobs for one, two or four weeks from your dashboard. If you let {{ cfg.AUTO_PAUSE_AFTER }} jobs in a row run out without quoting, we pause them for you so customers aren’t left waiting.

What do I need to join?

Your business name, the trades you do, the areas you cover and — if your work needs one — your licence or registration number. NZBN and insurance details help your quotes stand out.
