Good questions

Does it cost me anything?

No. Posting a job, getting quotes and messaging tradies is free for you.

Will I get hounded with calls?

No. Tradies see your suburb and the job — not your name, number or address. You share your details only with the ones you want to talk to.

What if nobody quotes?

Every day, spots that weren’t used go to other local tradies. That keeps going for {{ cfg.JOB_OPEN_DAYS }} days, or until you’ve got six quotes.

How do I know they’re legit?

Tradies give us their licence, NZBN and insurance details and we check them. You’ll only see a tick once we have — and it tells you what we checked and when.
